### 📖 Detailed Background

<table>
<tr>
<td valign="top">

With over **16 years in software engineering**, I've progressed from writing production code at **FPT Software** (2010–2015) to architecting enterprise systems at **NTQ Solution** (2018–2023), and now designing cutting-edge platforms at **Samsung SDS** (2023–present) in **Supply Chain Management** (SCM).

I specialize in **software architecture** — designing scalable, resilient distributed systems handling high-throughput workloads. I define technical roadmaps, evaluate architecture trade-offs (monolith vs microservices, event-driven vs request-response), select cloud infrastructure (AWS, Docker, CI/CD), and ensure systems are built for performance, reliability, and maintainability at scale.

Beyond architecture, I bring deep domain expertise in **Supply Chain Management**, **algorithmic trading**, and **quantitative finance**. Combined with entrepreneurial experience from founding my own company (2015–2018), I solve problems at the intersection of business, technology, and strategy.
